# CRM Data Analysis for Online Programming School

This project is a Python-based data analysis of CRM data for an online programming school. The goal is to clean, explore, and analyze sales and marketing data to improve business performance and identify growth opportunities.

## Project Goal
The main goal is to perform data cleaning, exploration, and analysis of CRM data to enhance the effectiveness of the sales and marketing processes of the online school. The project includes:

- Cleaning and preparing raw data  
- Descriptive statistical analysis  
- Temporal analysis of deals and calls  
- Evaluation of marketing campaigns and sales team performance  
- Analysis of payments, products, and geography  
- Formulation of business growth hypotheses and recommendations  

## Python Data Analysis Tasks

### Data Cleaning and Preparation
- Remove duplicates and irrelevant columns  
- Handle missing values appropriately  
- Convert data types (dates, numeric values) for analysis  

### Descriptive Statistics
- Calculate summary statistics: mean, median, mode, range for numeric fields  
- Analyze categorical fields: deal quality, stage, source, and product  

### Time Series Analysis
- Study trends of deal creation over time and their relation to calls  
- Examine the distribution of deal closure times and duration from creation to closure  

### Marketing Campaign Analysis
- Compare the effectiveness of different campaigns in generating leads and conversion rates  
- Evaluate which marketing sources produce the most high-quality leads  

### Sales Performance Analysis
- Assess individual deal owners and campaigns by number of deals processed, conversion rate, and total sales amount  

### Payment and Product Analysis
- Study distribution of payment types and their effect on deal success  
- Analyze popularity and success of different products and types of courses  

### Geographic Analysis
- Examine deal distribution by cities  
- Explore how German language proficiency affects deal success across regions  

---

## Product Analytics Tasks
- Calculate unit economics per product  
- Identify business growth points based on unit economics  
- Map out the metric tree for the business  
- Determine which product metric to influence and formulate hypotheses  
- Describe hypothesis testing methodology for a test period of no more than 2 weeks
